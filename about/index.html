Idaho Five Star Funnel Cake is a family-owned mobile food business based in Boise, Idaho, specializing in funnel cakes and festival favorites for fairs, festivals, rodeos and private events.

01 / Our story

Our story.

Youssef & Omar Ebed

Owners & operators

My name is Youssef Ebed, and my brother Omar and I own and operate Idaho Five Star Funnel Cake and Aladdin's Egyptian Cuisine.

We grew up working in both businesses alongside our parents, gaining hands-on experience in the food service industry from a young age. While both attending college, we decided to take over the businesses and make them our own.

Our focus is on modernizing operations, improving the customer experience, expanding into new events and locations, and continuing to grow both businesses while maintaining their family-owned identity.
